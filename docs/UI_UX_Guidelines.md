# 📘 ParthMind UI/UX Design Guidelines

## 1. Introduction
ParthMind is a mental wellness web app designed to provide **comfort, support, and personalized insights** through AI-powered chat, affirmations, music, and activities.  
The UI/UX prioritizes **calmness, clarity, and ease of navigation**.

### Goals
- Provide a **calming, supportive environment** for users.
- Create an **intuitive, minimal, and engaging interface**.
- Ensure **consistency** across devices with responsive design.
- Maintain **accessibility** for users with different needs.

---

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font**: Roboto (Sans-serif) / Open Sans
- **Font Sizes**:
  - H1: 32px
  - H2: 24px
  - H3: 20px
  - Body: 16px
  - Captions/Notes: 12–14px
- **Font Weight**:
  - Headings: Bold
  - Body: Regular/Medium
- **Line Spacing**: 1.5x font size for readability
- **Affirmations**: Italic or slightly stylized for warmth

---

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary Color**: `#4A90E2` (Calm Blue – buttons, highlights)
- **Secondary Color**: `#A29BFE` (Soft Lavender – accents)
- **Accent Color**: `#55EFC4` (Mint Green – highlights)
- **Background**:
  - Light Mode: `#FFFFFF`
  - Dark Mode: `#1C1C2E`
- **Text**:
  - Primary: `#2D3436`
  - Secondary: `#636E72`
- **System Colors**:
  - Success: `#4CAF50`
  - Error: `#F44336`

---

## 4. Component Styling

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Filled with primary color, white text
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined with primary color, black text
- **Border Radius**: 8–12px
- **Padding**: 12px 20px
- **States**: Default, Hover, Active, Disabled
- **Primary CTA**: Gradient (blue → teal)

### Cards
- Background: White
- Shadow: Subtle (0px 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1))
- Padding: 16px
- Rounded corners: 12px
- Used for: Features (Chat, Songs, Affirmations, Activities, AI Insights, Emotion Diary)

### Navigation
- **Top Bar**: Logo (left), Log In / Sign Up (right)
- Sticky header for accessibility

### Footer
- Links: About, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service
- Centered copyright

---

## 5. Spacing & Alignment
- **Grid System**: 12-column grid
- **Spacing Scale**:
  - XS: 4px
  - S: 8px
  - M: 16px
  - L: 24px
  - XL: 32px
- **Alignment**:
  - Text → Left-aligned
  - Buttons/CTAs → Center-aligned

---

## 6. Icons & Imagery
- **Icons**:
  - Material Icons or Emoji-style
  - Standard size: 24px, Emphasis: 32px
- **Images**:
  - Rounded corners: 8px
  - Consistent aspect ratios
  - Support light/dark backgrounds

---

## 7. User Flow

### Landing Page
- **Sections**:
  - Navigation (Logo, Login, Sign Up)
  - Hero Section: Tagline, Call-to-Action (Try It / Sign Up)
  - Features Grid (Chat, Songs, Activities, Affirmations, AI Insights, Emotion Diary)
  - Closing CTA: *“Ready to start your journey with ParthMind?”* → **Sign Up**
  - Footer

### Sign Up / Log In
- Minimal, centered card UI
- Sign Up: Name, Email, Password, Confirm Password
- Log In: Email, Password
- Social login (Google, Apple)

### Dashboard (After Login)
- Sidebar Navigation:
  - Chat Support
  - Songs
  - Activities
  - Affirmations
  - AI Insights
  - Emotion Diary
- Main Area:
  - Greeting: *“Hi [User], how are you feeling today?”*
  - Daily affirmation
  - Quick action cards

### Feature Pages
- **Chat Support**: Calm chat bubbles, AI responses
- **Song Recommendations**: Playlist with moods (Happy, Relax, Focus)
- **Activities**: Suggestion cards, completion button
- **Affirmations**: Daily affirmation, "Generate New"
- **AI Insights**: Emotional analysis, charts/visuals
- **Emotion Diary**: Journaling editor, save & track entries

---

## 8. Accessibility Guidelines
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1
- Font size: minimum 16px
- Dark mode toggle
- Screen reader support (semantic HTML)
- Keyboard navigation + visible focus states

---

## 9. Interaction & Animation Guidelines
- Smooth transitions (0.3s)
- Hover effects for buttons/links
- Fade-in for cards
- Slide-in for chat messages
- Calming, non-intrusive animations

---

## 10. Responsive Design
- **Mobile-first approach**
- Breakpoints:
  - Small (≤600px): Stacked layout
  - Medium (601–1024px): 2-column
  - Large (≥1025px): Grid layout

---

## 11. Future Enhancements
- Voice chat support
- Mood tracking with AI-powered analytics
- Spotify/YouTube integration
- Gamification (badges for journaling consistency)
