## 🌟 Our Pledge

We, as contributors, maintainers, and participants of *ParthMind*, pledge to create a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for everyone. ParthMind is a mental health support platform, designed to help users manage overthinking and depression. Therefore, our community must reflect empathy, kindness, and respect in all interactions.  

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that foster an open, supportive, and respectful environment. Every voice and perspective is valued, and we encourage collaboration, mentorship, and knowledge-sharing among contributors. We listen actively, respond with empathy, and take responsibility for our actions. Integrity, honesty, and professionalism guide all contributions and interactions.  

Together, we strive to create a community where contributors feel inspired to share ideas, learn, and help others flourish. By following this pledge, we ensure that ParthMind remains a collaborative, empathetic, and respectful space for all contributors.

---

## 🚦 Our Standards

Positive contributions include, but are not limited to:

- 🤝 Showing respect and empathy for others’ ideas, experiences, and mental health perspectives
- 📝 Offering constructive feedback and accepting suggestions gracefully
- 🌱 Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- 🎯 Prioritizing actions that benefit the project and its community
- 🌍 Supporting newcomers and helping them get started
- 🧠 Encouraging a safe, kind, and considerate online environment

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or offensive remarks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sharing content that could negatively impact mental health

---

## 👨‍⚖ Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers ensure that community standards are upheld. They may take appropriate actions if behavior is considered harmful, offensive, or inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.  

Maintainers are responsible for managing contributions, discussions, and other project interactions to maintain a positive and empathetic community atmosphere.

---

## 🌐 Scope

ParthMind’s Code of Conduct applies to all participants, including contributors, maintainers, moderators, and anyone interacting with the project’s resources. This includes all interactions in the project’s repositories, forums, chat platforms, and any representation of the project in public spaces.

## 🌈 Our Community Values

ParthMind thrives on the following shared values:

- 🤝 Respect – Every contributor’s voice matters
- 💡 Curiosity – Questions and exploration are encouraged
- 🌱 Growth – Mistakes are learning opportunities
- ❤ Empathy – We engage thoughtfully and kindly
- 🔄 Collaboration – We work together and share credit

## 📌 Contribution Behavior

All contributions should align with this Code of Conduct. Contributors are encouraged to:

- 🚫 Avoid content or actions that could be offensive, discriminatory, or harmful to mental health
- 🤝 Accept feedback and respond constructively
- 🧘 Stay composed during conflicts or disagreements
- 🛠 Celebrate collaboration and team achievements
- 📚 Embrace learning and help others develop
- 🤲 Focus on teamwork rather than competition

---

## 🛡 Enforcement

Concerns about inappropriate behavior can be reported confidentially to the project maintainers through the appropriate platform channels.

All reports are taken seriously and handled fairly to ensure the safety and well-being of all contributors.

---

## 🤫 Confidentiality & Non-Retaliation

All reports remain confidential. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited.

---

## 📖 Enforcement Guidelines

Project leaders determine consequences based on the severity of the issue:

1. ✏ Minor Issue  
   Impact: Small or unintentional behavior concerns  
   Consequence: Private discussion and clarification; public apology may be requested if necessary

2. ⚠ Warning  
   Impact: Clear violation or repeated minor issues  
   Consequence: Official warning; temporary limitations on participation if repeated

3. ⏳ Temporary Restriction  
   Impact: Sustained inappropriate behavior  
   Consequence: Temporary restriction from contributing

4. 🚫 Permanent Restriction  
   Impact: Severe or repeated violations, including harassment or hate speech  
   Consequence: Permanent ban from project participation
